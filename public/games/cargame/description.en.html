How to Play Racing Car: A Fun Guide for Kids

Are you ready to zoom into action? Here’s how you can become a racing car master in just a few easy steps:

Controls Made Simple

  • On a computer: Use the left and right arrow keys to steer. Press the up arrow to go faster and the down arrow to slow down.
  • On a tablet or phone: Swipe right or left to turn, swipe up to speed up, and swipe down to slow down.

Race Smart, Not Just Fast

  • Watch for sharp bends! Slow down a bit before you turn, then speed up as you exit the corner for the best lap times.
  • Keep an eye out for slick oil patches or walls—hitting them can really slow you down.

Collect Coins and Power-Ups

  • The more you collect, the more awesome cars and new tracks you unlock.
