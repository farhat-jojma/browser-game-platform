Controls

Dive into Cooking Street, the ultimate cooking game for kids available right in your web browser, using these easy and fun controls!

Slide between stations: Just use the left (←) and right (→) arrow keys to move around the kitchen.

Hop over obstacles: Jump over slippery puddles and buzzing helper robots by pressing the up arrow (↑) or the spacebar.

Grab your ingredients: Click on the fruits, veggies, or spices to collect them, then drag each ingredient into your pots, pans, or plates.

Serve your dishes: Send your delicious creations to the diners by clicking the bright Serve button or tapping the S key.

No need for downloads or installs—everything runs directly in your web browser. Now, put on your chef’s hat and start a fun-filled cooking adventure!
