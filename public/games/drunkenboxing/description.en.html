How to Play Drunken Boxing

Are you ready for a wobbly showdown in the ring? Here's a simple guide to help you become a master of Drunken Boxing—perfect for young gamers!

1. Move and Groove

Use the left/right arrows (or A and D) to shuffle your boxer back and forth. Wiggle around your opponent to dodge incoming blows and find the perfect angle for your own punches.

2. Duck and Dive

Press the up arrow (or W) to bend down under a high punch. Timing is key—duck too early or too late, and you might get tapped!

3. Punch Power

Hit the spacebar to throw a playful jab at your foe. Land three or more in a row to rack up combo points and fill your “Tipsy Meter.”

4. Unleash Your Special Move

When the Tipsy Meter glows full, press down (or S) plus spacebar at the same time. Watch your boxer spin, stagger, and deliver a super-drunk blow that can send anyone flying!

5. Keep an Eye on Your Health

The green bar at the top shows your remaining stamina. If it hits zero, you’ll wobble out of the match—so dodge smart and strike fast!

6. Claim the Champion’s Belt

Battle through a colorful cast of silly fighters. Outsmart their moves, out-drunk their tricks, and ring the final bell as the undisputed champion!

Tips for Tiny Fighters

Practice sliding in and out quickly to surprise your opponent. Mix light taps (spacebar) with special spins (down+space) for the slickest combos. Keep smiling—Drunken Boxing is all about fun, wobble power, and friendly competition!
