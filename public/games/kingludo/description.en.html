How to Play Ludo King

Welcome to this kid-friendly Ludo King guide! In just a few steps, you’ll learn how to set up the board, move your tokens, and outsmart your friends in this classic family game. Let’s dive in!

Choose Your Color

Pick one of the four bright colors: red, blue, green, or yellow. Place all four matching tokens inside your home circle. That’s your starting zone.

Roll to Begin

Take turns rolling a single die. Only a roll of six lets you launch one token from home onto the first square. Can’t roll a six? No worries—pass the die to the next player and try again on your next turn.

Move Around the Track

On your turn, roll the die and move any one of your tokens forward exactly that many spaces. Follow the shaped path around the board, always staying on the colored route.

Bump Opponents Back

Land on a square that’s already occupied by someone else? You “bump” their token right back to its home circle. Watch out—if another player lands on your token, you’ll get bumped, too!

Safe Zones and Shortcuts

Each colored stretch leading to your finishing triangle is a safe zone. Tokens here cannot be bumped, so strategize to reach this lane quickly.

Winning the Race

The first player to guide all four tokens from home, around the board, and into their matching home triangle wins the game. Celebrate your victory and get ready for a rematch!

Tips for Tiny Champions

  • Team up with a buddy for your first few games.
  • Count each move out loud to build number skills.
  • Cheer for everyone—Ludo King is all about having fun together!

With this easy Ludo King tutorial, you’ll be rolling, racing, and bumping like a pro. Gather your friends, grab the dice, and let the best roller win!

 

Controls
