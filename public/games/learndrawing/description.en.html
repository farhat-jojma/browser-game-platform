Jump into the colorful toolbox and select a paintbrush, crayon, or marker. Want to erase a stray line? The eraser is right there. Switch tools anytime to try new styles.

5. Earn Stars & Unlock More

Every time you finish an artwork, you collect stars that unlock new and awesome images. The more you draw, the more creative challenges await, helping you sharpen your drawing skills every day.

Want to draw your own shapes? Just click and drag your mouse. If you want to erase something, just right-click your mouse. If you need to start over, press R to reset and try again.
